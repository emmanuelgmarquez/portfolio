Your list is getting every email twice.

On November 13 I got the same job announcement twice, ten seconds apart, from two different sending domains. They came from two separate ClickFunnels accounts, each with its own list and its own unsubscribe link.

Why it costs money

Spam complaints. Getting the same email twice is a common reason people hit "report spam." Gmail and Yahoo use complaint rates to decide whether your mail lands in the inbox, Promotions, or spam, for every send.

Unsubscribes that don't stick. Someone who unsubscribes from one list keeps getting your mail from the other, which drives even more complaints.

Numbers you can't trust. Each account counts the same person separately, so opens and clicks look bigger than your real audience.

How I'd fix it

  1. Match the lists. Export both accounts and find everyone who is on both.
  2. One shared do-not-email list. Sync unsubscribes between the two accounts, so leaving one means leaving both.
  3. One send for group news. Company-wide emails go out once, from one domain, to everyone combined.
